Support

Pitch Life for iOS and Android · Last updated 11 November 2026

One person reads this mailbox: vibalyze@zohomail.eu. Write in English or Turkish. Expect a reply within a few days.

There is no support account to create and no ticket system. Email it, or from version 1.0.3 use Settings → “Write to us” in the game (add your email there if you want a reply).

Reporting a bug

The game is deterministic: the same career, the same week and the same decisions produce the same match, every time, on every phone. That means a well-described bug can usually be reproduced exactly — but only if the report says where in the career it happened. Please include:

  1. Your device and OS version — for example "Pixel 7, Android 15" or "iPhone 13, iOS 18.4".
  2. The app version, from the store page for Pitch Life.
  3. Your player's full name and club, exactly as the game spells them. Both are generated, so together they identify your career.
  4. The player's age and the season, and which screen you were on: the match, full time, training, the transfer window, a story event, the league table.
  5. What you tapped, what happened, and what you expected. If it was a match decision, name the choice you picked.
  6. A screenshot or a screen recording. For anything visual or anything mid-match, this is worth more than the other five points put together.

If the game crashed, say whether it crashed once or every time, and whether it still crashes after force-quitting and reopening. From version 1.0.3 the game sends an anonymous error report when its own code hits an error (the kind of error and where, never its message; see the privacy policy), but a report from you still tells us far more.

Careers and save files

Each career is a single JSON file inside the app's own private storage, in a saves folder. The game writes to a temporary file and renames it over the real one, and keeps the previous version alongside as .bak, so a crash in the middle of a save leaves you with the last good career rather than a broken one. Saves are written after every match and after every major decision.

Getting a career off the phone

Settings shows Export career and Import career buttons. In the current build they are placeholders and do nothing when tapped. The code that produces the export exists and is tested, but it is not yet connected to those buttons, so there is currently no way to copy a career out of the game or move one to another phone yourself.

When they are connected, the flow will be: Settings → Export career → the career is handed to your phone's share sheet as a JSON file, which you can save or mail to yourself. Importing takes that same file back and writes it into a career slot. Until then, please do not count on moving a career between devices.

If you lose a career

There is no cloud save and no account, so we hold no copy and cannot restore a career. The two things that protect a career are your phone's backup, and not tapping through the overwrite warning:

  • Saves live in the app's documents folder, which is included in Android Auto Backup and in iOS device and iCloud backups. Reinstalling the app on a phone with backup enabled normally brings the career back. Keep backup switched on.
  • New career on the title screen overwrites the career already in that slot. The game names the career you are about to lose and asks you to confirm. Once you confirm, it is gone.

Deleting everything

Uninstall the app. That removes the save files, the index and the random install ID with it. Your career is never stored on a server. The anonymous statistics (version 1.0.3 and later, only sent if you said yes when the game asked) and any message you sent us can be erased too: email us and we will help you find your install. If you also want the copy inside your phone's backup gone, clear it from your device's backup settings.

Things that are working as intended

Low energy never stops you playing

Energy limits how hard you can train and it makes you play worse and pick up injuries more easily, but it never locks a match behind a timer. If the Play match button is there, you can play. It always is.

The clubs and players are not real

Every club, league, stadium, teammate and rival is invented by the game and checked against a blocklist of real clubs and real people before it appears. If a generated name still looks like a real club or a real person, that is a bug — please report it and it will be added to the blocklist.

The AI players around you are characters

The rivals in the news and the award races are written by the game. They are not other human players, and the game will never claim they are.

You do not pick the team

You control one footballer. You earn minutes or you sit on the bench, and that is the game rather than a fault in it.

Known limitations in the current build

  • Export and import do nothing (see above).
  • Settings are not remembered. The haptics switch, the analytics switch and the matches-per-season slider reset when you leave the screen.
  • The language buttons in Settings do not change the language. The game follows your device language: set your phone to English or Turkish and Pitch Life will follow it.
  • There is no in-game delete button for a single career. Uninstalling, or starting a new career in that slot, is the way to remove one.

Feature requests

Welcome, and genuinely read. The most useful ones name the moment that felt flat rather than the feature: "the match after I got dropped read exactly like the one before it" is more actionable than a list.
