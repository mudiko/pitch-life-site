Privacy policy

Pitch Life for iOS and Android · Last updated 11 November 2026 (anonymous statistics, error reports and “Write to us” from version 1.0.3)

Pitch Life collects no personal data. It has no accounts and no ads, it never asks who you are, and your career is saved as ordinary files on your own phone. From version 1.0.3 it sends a few anonymous gameplay statistics so we can see which parts of the game work, and you can switch them off in the game’s Settings. If you write to us from the game, we receive your message. Versions up to 1.0.2 send nothing at all.

What the game sends (version 1.0.3 and later)

Anonymous usage statistics. The game sends short gameplay events to our own server, for example “career started”, “match played, rating 7.4, one goal”, “moment played: shot, scored”, “training day”, “story card answered”, “transfer accepted” or “season ended in third place”. Each event holds numbers and fixed labels chosen by the game, plus the app version, the operating system and its version, your phone’s language and time zone offset, and the country of the connection.

  • Events are tied only to a random install ID made on your phone (and a random ID per session). It is not your name, not your Apple or Google account, not a device identifier and not the advertising identifier. A new random ID replaces it after 13 months.
  • The name you type for your player, your club’s name and anything else you type are never part of an event.
  • The server derives the country from the connection; it never stores or logs your IP address.
  • Raw events are deleted after 120 days. Daily totals that hold no IDs are kept for 25 months.
  • Offline, events wait on the phone (at most 1,000) and are sent later. The game works fully without a connection.
  • Turn it off any time: Settings → “Share anonymous analytics”. Off deletes the events still waiting and nothing more is sent.

Anonymous error reports. If the game hits a software error, it sends an “error” event with the kind of error, a short fingerprint, and the names of the game’s own code files and lines where it happened, so we can find and fix the bug. The error’s message is never sent (it could contain something you typed), and nor is anything else from your phone. These reports go through the same anonymous statistics, with the same install ID, retention and off switch.

Where the install came from (iPhone only). On first launch the game asks Apple’s AdServices framework for a token and sends it to our server once, so we can learn whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign (campaign, ad group and keyword, the country and the date). This is Apple’s privacy-preserving attribution: it contains no advertising identifier and nothing about you, it is not tracking, and the game shows no tracking prompt. If statistics are off, nothing is sent.

“Write to us”. If you send us a message from the game’s Settings, we receive the message, its type (bug, idea or other), the app and system details above, the install ID (so we can match a bug to what happened), and your email address only if you choose to give one so we can reply. Messages are kept for 24 months. This works even when statistics are off, because you send it on purpose.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, with the database in the European Union. Cloudflare processes the data for us as a service provider. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

What we never collect

No name, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone, no camera, no advertising identifier and no device identifier. The app contains no advertising libraries, no third-party analytics or crash reporting service, no in-app purchase libraries and no social or sign-in integrations. On Android, version 1.0.3 adds the INTERNET permission for the statistics and “Write to us” above, and nothing else uses it. The other system features the app uses are the one that tells it where its own private storage folder is, so it can write your save file there, and the two described below.

Match reminders

If you choose to turn them on, the app can schedule one reminder on your phone about your next match, at most once a day. It is off until you tap “Remind me” or switch “Match reminders” on in Settings, and only then does your phone ask for notification permission. The reminder is a local notification: the app writes its text on your phone from your own career and hands it to your phone's operating system to show later. There is no push service, no notification token, and nothing about it is sent to us or anyone else. Turn it off any time in the game's Settings or your phone's settings.

Store ratings

Occasionally, after a high point in a career, the app asks your phone to show Apple's or Google's own rating sheet. Whether it appears, and anything you write in it, is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. The app is never told whether you rated it.

What the app stores on your device

One file per career, plus a small index of which careers exist. They are written inside the app's own documents folder:

  • saves/slot_0.json — a career, as plain readable JSON
  • saves/slot_0.json.bak — the previous version of that career, kept so a crash during a save cannot cost you the file
  • saves/index.json — the short summary shown on the title screen (player name, club, age, when it was last played)

A career file contains only game state: the name you typed for your player, their position, attributes, money, form and morale, the generated clubs and league they play in, which story events have already happened, and timestamps of when the save was written. All of it is generated by the game or typed by you into the game. The save file is never transmitted.

Small files in the app’s support folder hold the random install ID, your statistics on/off choice and the events waiting to be sent.

The game's content — commentary lines, story events, club and city names, the lifestyle catalogue — ships inside the app as bundled files. Nothing is downloaded at runtime, so there is no request to a content server and nothing to log.

Device backups

Because saves live in the app's documents folder, they are picked up by your phone's normal backup: Android Auto Backup on Android, and device or iCloud backup on iOS. That is deliberate, so that reinstalling the app does not delete a ten-season career. Those backups are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies and under your own backup settings. We cannot see them, and we have no way to read your save.

Accounts, online play, leaderboards

There are none. There is no login, no friends list, no chat, no cloud save and no online leaderboard in this version. The rival players you read about in the game are characters the game invents; they are not other people and they are never presented as other people. The entire game works with the phone offline; the statistics simply wait until a connection is back.

If online features are ever added, this page will be updated before they ship and the store listing's privacy answers will be redone to match.

Advertising and purchases

The app currently contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. There is no ad network in the build, so there is no ad profiling, no tracking prompt and nothing to opt out of. There is a single in-game currency, which is earned by playing and cannot be bought.

The statistics switch in Settings

Up to version 1.0.2, Settings showed an Analytics switch that was off by default and not connected to anything, because the app had no analytics. From version 1.0.3 the switch, “Share anonymous analytics”, controls the anonymous statistics and error reports described above. They hold no personal data and no identifier that can be tied to a person, so they are on by default; switching them off is one tap, stops all sending at once and deletes what was waiting.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Pitch Life deletes the app's documents and support folders, and with them every save file, the index, the install ID and any events still waiting to be sent. Turning statistics off stops all sending at once. To have the statistics and messages from your install deleted from our server, email us; we will help you find your install and erase it.

Two things to know:

  • Your phone's backup may still hold a copy of the save after you uninstall. Clearing that is done in your device's backup settings, not in the game.
  • Starting a new career from the title screen overwrites the existing career in that slot. The game asks you to confirm first and names the career you are about to lose, but once confirmed it is gone.

Settings also shows Export career and Import career buttons. In the current build these are placeholders and do nothing when tapped. When they work, an export will be a plain JSON copy of one career that you choose where to send — we will not receive it.

Children

Pitch Life is not directed at young children, but no part of that matters much here: the app collects no personal data from anyone, of any age. There are no ads, no purchases, no chat, no user profiles and no way for one player to see another player's content. The anonymous statistics identify no one, and nothing a child types into the game leaves the device unless they send it through “Write to us”.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child sends us a message with an email address, a parent can ask us to delete it. If you believe something in the app contradicts this page, please write to us at the address below and we will fix it.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the GDPR gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Estonia, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon). The statistics are anonymous and we cannot link them to you on our own, but if you give us your install’s details or the email you wrote from, we will act on any request. Your save file is yours, on your device, and you can read it with any text editor.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something this page does not describe — ads, purchases, cloud save — this page is updated first, with a new date at the top, and the store privacy forms are updated with it. We will not quietly add collection under an old policy.
